# Governance

This project is open source, but it is not directionless.

The goal is to keep things transparent, fair, and aligned with the purpose of the platform without turning it into a committee-driven mess.

---

## The model

This project follows a simple model:

Open contribution, curated direction.

Anyone can contribute. Not everything gets merged.

---

## Maintainer

The maintainer is responsible for:

- Product direction
- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Protecting the core principles
- Ensuring safety and privacy are not compromised

The maintainer has final say on decisions.

---

## Why this model

Open contribution without clear direction leads to fragmentation and complexity.

This project is trying to reduce fragmentation, not create more of it.

---

## Core principles

These are not up for debate:

1. Privacy over convenience  
2. Safety over growth  
3. Hosts must be the actual host of the activity  
4. No one owns the community  
5. Keep it simple  

If something conflicts with these, it does not get implemented.

---

## Contributors

Contributors can:

- Submit pull requests
- Suggest improvements
- Raise issues

Contributors cannot:

- Override core principles
- Force direction through volume or pressure

---

## Transparency

- Major decisions should be explained publicly (via issues or docs)
- Moderation logic should be visible where it affects public content
- No hidden systems that impact users without visibility

---

## Community input

Feedback is encouraged and considered.

Good ideas can come from anywhere.

However, input is not the same as control.

---

## Future structure (optional)

As the project grows, a small advisory group may be formed.

- 3-5 people max
- Public discussions
- Advisory only

Final decisions remain with the maintainer.

---

## What this is trying to avoid

- Centralised control of communities
- Hidden moderation
- Feature bloat
- Politics and gatekeeping

---

## Final point

This is a tool for communities, not a platform that owns them.

The structure reflects that.
